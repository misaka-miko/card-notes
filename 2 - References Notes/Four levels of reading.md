# Four levels of reading
Generally, reading can be "divided" into four levels. It is worth to mention that the lower level is not separate from the upper level but involved in the upper ones.
1. The first level is *Elementary reading*, which refers to knowing what a particular sentence means, which could already happen in our early ages. But this level can occasionally happen when we read books written in foreign language.
2. The second level is *Inspectional reading*, or in other words *Skimming reading*. This level of reading emphasizes time, and readers are required to infer the main idea of the book in limited time.
3. The third level is *Analytical reading*, which requires the reader to seriously read a book thoroughly. This level is suitable for those want to get true insight from the book.
4. The fourth level is *Syntopical reading*, or *Comparative reading*. This is the highest level of reading, requiring not only to read one book, but more than one book. Readers should inspect their relationship and generate new ideas or topics which are not mentioned among the books.
