# multitasking is not a good idea

Many young people claimed that they are **multi-taskers**, but in fact, they are not, and they didn't really understand what is multi-tasking and whether they are really good at it.

First, the multi-taskers don't actually have a control group on the working efficiency measurement, which leads to the illusion that they are good at it. Secondly, **familiarity** on multi-tasking doesn't equal to good at it. The mental feeling is independent of our actual performance, which is *mere-exposure effect*.

Therefore, what we can do in writing is dividing it into different tasks and focusing on one thing at a time. A structured workflow is necessary for better focusing.
