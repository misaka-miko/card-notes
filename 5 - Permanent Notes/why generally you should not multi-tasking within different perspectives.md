# why generally you should not multi-tasking within different perspectives

Why you shouldn't multi-tasking when you working? Or at least you shouldn't switch your task too often. The answer is short: the overhead or switching task is high.

Multi-tasking involves what I refer as **context switch**, which itself is a term used in operating system. It basically means when the scheduler needs to switch to another thread (*similar to doing another job*), it has to store the current thread's running state, including register values, execution flags and etc and restore the incoming thread's state from memory into core. The process is actually expensive, so the scheduler shouldn't switch too often, or you will have your operating system switching task all the time without actually finish any task.

Human's attention is also nothing too special, when we focusing on one task, the content of the task is in our short-term memory, we have a clear picture of what we are doing. Things change when you choose to do another task, the content in your short-term memory is evicted and replaced by other content, if you didn't have any progress on that before, then you will have to spend much more effort to get into it. That is the damage to your workflow, and things get even worse when you try to go back to previous work, as you switch task without storing your ideas, you are facing the risk of redoing what you have done before.
